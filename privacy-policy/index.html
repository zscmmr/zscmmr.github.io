Privacy Policy

This page explains what information may be processed when you visit the zscmmr portfolio website and how this site uses third-party resources.

Information handled by the site

This is a static portfolio website. It does not provide account registration, checkout, or an on-site contact form, and it does not intentionally use analytics or advertising trackers. The hosting provider, GitHub Pages, may process technical request information such as IP address, browser details, and access time to deliver and protect the website. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement for information about its handling of data.

Third-party resources

Pages may load typefaces from Google Fonts and icons from the unpkg content delivery network. When your browser requests these resources, the providers may receive technical information such as your IP address. Their processing is governed by their own privacy policies and service terms.

Email and external websites

Email links open your email application or service; information you choose to send is handled by that provider and the recipient. Project demonstrations and social links lead to external websites that are not controlled by this site. Review those sites’ privacy information before sharing personal data with them.

Updates and questions

This notice may be updated when the website or its services change. For questions about this page, contact nexbyte.wrld@gmail.com.
